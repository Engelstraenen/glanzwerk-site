# GLANZWERK – Video-Prompts (Drafts gratis, Endrender nur MiniMax H3 2K nach OK, ≤15 s)
Regeln: One-Take, nur First Frame (4K) als Referenz, Vorher → Arbeit → Ergebnis, jeder auf der Seite genannte Schritt sichtbar.

## V1 buero (Start: GW-01 nacht-buero) – Innen 22:00 → 01:40, 12 s
One continuous locked-off wide shot from a fixed camera at standing eye height, NO cuts, the camera stays still with a very slow gentle push forward. Layout: long modern open-plan office at night, rows of white desks left and right, dull light grey stone floor in the middle, floor-to-ceiling windows with blue night city lights. Time-lapse of a professional night cleaning, the light stays night the whole time.
0–2 s: the empty messy office as in the first frame: cups, papers and full bins on the desks, dirty footprints on the dull floor.
2–6 s: fast time-lapse: two cleaning technicians in graphite work suits with mint green details move quickly between the desks with a modern cleaning trolley, cups and papers disappear, bins are emptied, desks are wiped, chairs are pushed neatly under the desks.
6–11 s: one technician guides a professional single-disc floor scrubbing machine in slow straight lanes across the stone floor from the back of the room towards the camera; behind the machine every lane turns into a deep glossy mirror finish that reflects the ceiling lights and the city lights.
11–12 s: the technicians are gone, the office is perfectly tidy, the whole floor shines like a mirror.
Style: photorealistic, cinematic, true-to-life colors, natural motion, subtle film grain. Avoid: cuts, camera jumps, morphing people or furniture, people appearing from nowhere, text, logos, CGI look.

## V2 hof (Start: GW-02 hof-vorher) – Außen 02:00, 12 s
One continuous locked-off top-down drone shot from 40 m height, NO cuts, the drone hovers perfectly still. Layout as in the first frame: huge courtyard of dark dirty concrete paving slabs with moss, four floodlight towers at the corners, two technicians in graphite work suits with mint green details with round rotating high-pressure surface cleaners at one short edge.
0–1 s: still frame, the technicians start walking.
1–11 s: time-lapse: the two technicians push the round surface cleaners in perfectly straight parallel lanes across the whole courtyard, each lane they leave behind is bright light-grey and clean, the clean stripes grow side by side until the whole courtyard is bright and clean, a little water shine on the slabs.
11–12 s: the whole courtyard is clean and bright, the technicians stand at the edge.
Style: photorealistic, true-to-life colors, natural motion. Avoid: cuts, camera movement, morphing, extra people, text, logos, CGI look.

## V3 fassade (Start: GW-03 fassade) – Glas 05:00, 10 s
One continuous shot, NO cuts, the camera slowly drifts upward along the facade. Layout as in the first frame: dusty grey glass curtain wall at blue hour, rope access technician on two ropes in the right third, squeegee in hand.
0–3 s: the technician wets the dusty glass with a washing applicator, foam runs down.
3–8 s: he pulls the stainless steel squeegee in long horizontal strokes across the glass, every stroke leaves a crystal clear stripe that reflects the pink dawn sky, he lowers himself slowly on the ropes and continues.
8–10 s: a large area of glass is perfectly clear and mirrors the dawn sky, the sky gets brighter.
Style: photorealistic, true-to-life colors, real rope access equipment, natural motion. Avoid: cuts, morphing hands or tools, rope disappearing, text, logos, CGI look.

## V4 karriere (Start: GW-05 karriere) – Loop 6 s
Locked-off portrait shot, the young technician on the roof at sunrise breathes naturally, a light breeze moves her ponytail, she smiles slightly and looks into the camera, the sun rises slowly behind the skyline. No cuts, no camera movement. Photorealistic. Avoid: morphing face, text, logos.
